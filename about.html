01 — THE NSS

National Service
Scheme.

NSS provides students with opportunities to participate in community service and development activities while strengthening personality, leadership and social awareness.

At SLIET Longowal, NSS activities connect campus life with the wider community through outreach, awareness programmes, volunteering and service.
