01 — NSS AT SLIET

Learning beyond
the classroom.

The official SLIET NSS programme describes NSS as a platform for community service, personal development and student social awareness.

Its stated objectives include personality development through community service, participation in planning and development activities, and promoting dignity of labour and self-help under the motto “Not Me But You”.
